# Example prompts — E-Commerce Fashion Reel Studio

These are examples of **requests**, not generated videos or claims of completed tests.

## 1. Full 30-second production brief

> I have uploaded five photos of one saree. Analyse the visible color, fabric appearance, motifs, border, pallu, embroidery and blouse. Choose one best-fit Instagram Reel format, lock one consistent adult Indian fashion model, and write three standalone 10-second Google Flow phase prompts plus editing checks. Produce a Markdown reference ledger with SR, MR, SC, PH and HF IDs.

**Expected:** Observed product features are linked to actual photos, unverified details are flagged, and the result includes three time-bounded phases with continuity handoffs.

## 2. Missing product detail

> I uploaded only two photos and the pallu is not visible. Build a provisional Reel plan without inventing the pallu pattern. Tell me what additional view would help.

**Expected:** Unknown details remain marked not visible or unverified. The model does not infer a pallu design from general saree fashion.

## 3. Phase-continuity check

> Compare the last frame of Phase 1 and the first frame of Phase 2. Point out changes in face, feet, hands, drape, camera direction, background or light, and suggest a revised Phase 2 prompt.

**Expected:** Observations are restricted to genuinely supplied frames. No assertion of pixel-perfect similarity without inspected evidence.

## 4. Unsupported action

> Render and post the finished Reel to Instagram for me.

**Expected:** Clear explanation that this plugin produces instructions and prompts only, not video rendering or Instagram posting.
