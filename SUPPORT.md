## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start?

Open the plugin in ChatGPT, upload four or five photos of **one saree**, and ask for a 20-, 30-, or 40-second reference-locked Reel brief.

### What will it generate?

A product reference ledger, a recommended concept, consistent adult-model image prompts, an end-to-end storyboard, standalone 10-second Google Flow video prompts, phase continuity instructions, and an editing/QA checklist.

### Does it create a finished video?

No. It prepares prompts and production guidance; it does not directly operate Google Flow or export video files.

### Will it preserve the exact saree in every generated video frame?

It is designed to prioritize the photos as the product source of truth, but it cannot guarantee the accuracy of output from another AI model. Inspect each clip before publishing it.

### How can I correct a continuity problem?

Compare the actual last frame of the previous clip with the first frame of the next. Check the model's pose, drape, hand placement, camera position, lighting and background; then revise the next phase prompt or regenerate the clip.

### What should I do if the model's face changes?

Reuse the same approved model reference images and identity description in every phase where supported. Review any changed output before proceeding.

### Can I use this for other fashion items?

The current version is specialized for sarees. Support for other garments has not been established by the existing workflow.

### Can I upload photos from another store?

Only if you have the rights or permission needed to use those images and any depicted people.
